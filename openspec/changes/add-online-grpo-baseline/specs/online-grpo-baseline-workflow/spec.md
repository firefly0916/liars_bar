## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Online GRPO group rollout collection
The system SHALL collect online GRPO training data by running the current policy in the Liar's Bar environment against fixed mock opponents.

#### Scenario: Current policy generates grouped training actions
- **WHEN** an online GRPO update collects rollout games
- **THEN** player `p1` actions are generated by the current trainable policy and assigned to GRPO groups, while non-`p1` actions are generated by mock agents

### Requirement: Method-independent reward
The online GRPO baseline MUST NOT use CRC, proxy-best actions, EVGap, or HICRA token penalties as training rewards.

#### Scenario: Reward excludes CRC-GRA signals
- **WHEN** the online GRPO trainer computes rewards
- **THEN** rewards are derived from terminal outcome and protocol penalties only

### Requirement: Group-normalized objective
The online GRPO trainer SHALL compute group-normalized advantages and optimize a clipped sequence-level policy objective over LoRA policy parameters.

#### Scenario: GRPO update centers rewards within a group
- **WHEN** a group contains multiple generated decisions
- **THEN** the trainer subtracts the group mean reward before computing policy loss

### Requirement: Isolated 200-game formal evaluation
The online GRPO runner SHALL evaluate the final online GRPO adapter with 200 formal games by default under its own run root.

#### Scenario: Formal eval defaults to 200 games
- **WHEN** the online GRPO runner is invoked without overriding `FORMAL_GAMES`
- **THEN** it uses `FORMAL_GAMES=200` and writes Task M, audit, and scorecard artifacts under the online GRPO run root
